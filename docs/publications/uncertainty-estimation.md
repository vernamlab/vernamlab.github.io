This work introduces a Rényi entropy-based framework for measuring uncertainty in neural network-assisted side-channel analysis and explaining its effect on key recovery. It separates model-related and data-related uncertainty, uses matrix-based Rényi measures to approximate high-dimensional leakage distributions, and combines Shapley-value explanations with uncertainty estimates to identify influential trace features. Experiments show that predictive uncertainty strongly correlates with established attack metrics such as key rank.

*Nouraniboosjin, S., and Ganji, F.*
