Patrick Schaumont will serve as [Program Co-Chair of CRYPTO 2027](https://www.iacr.org/docs/minutes/minutes_bod_virtual-04-25.pdf) alongside Stefano Tessaro.

<!-- truncate -->

CRYPTO is one of the [flagship conferences of the International Association for Cryptologic Research (IACR)](https://crypto.iacr.org/2023/files/2023SponsorProspectus.pdf), bringing together cryptography researchers from academia and industry worldwide. His appointment continues a record of community service that includes [co-chairing CHES 2012](https://www.iacr.org/workshops/ches/ches2012/) and [serving on the CRYPTO program committees in 2016, 2023, and 2026](https://schaumont.wpi.edu/tpc.html).
