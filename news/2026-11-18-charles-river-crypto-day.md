Fatemeh (Saba) Ganji and members of her research team attended [Charles River Crypto Day](https://bostoncryptoday.wordpress.com/2026/09/28/friday-oct-2-at-bu/) at Boston University on October 2, 2026. The program featured researchers from NYU and MIT presenting advances in private information retrieval, quantum unclonable encryption, interactive proofs for AI safety, and proofs of space.
