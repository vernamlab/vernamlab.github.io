Fatemeh (Saba) Ganji and Shahin Tajik served as [Rump Session Chairs at CHES 2026](https://ches.iacr.org/2026/rumpsession.php) in Antalya, Türkiye. The October 13 session provided a forum for short, informal presentations of recent research and new ideas, with humorous and nontechnical contributions encouraged.
