# For advisors

Each request must be reviewed by the research advisor before resource scheduling. Advisors confirm that the proposed work is appropriate and supported; they do not allocate infrastructure themselves.

## Reviewing tickets

1. Open the request notification and its authorized review interface.
2. Check the research purpose, anticipated duration, workload category, and potential operational impact.
3. Approve or decline **each** ticket. If information is incomplete, ask the student to correct the request.
4. Where several future time slots are requested, review them **in one sitting** while recording a separate decision for each.

Advisor approval is required for **every reservation**, not merely once per project. Resource demands and risks may change as experiments evolve. The scheduling system handles routine approved requests, while administrators handle requests that require exclusive machine use or other intervention.

**Advisor approval alone does not authorize the student to run a job.** The student must receive a confirmed reservation for the specific time and resource allocation.
