# Usage policy

BEAST is reserved for authorized research use. All students, advisors, and approved collaborators are responsible for complying with the following rules.

1. **Request every job or defined experiment batch.** Use the official reservation process; informal messages are not substitutes.
2. **Obtain advisor approval per ticket.** Project-level approval alone is not sufficient.
3. **Wait for confirmed allocation.** Do not start work solely because an advisor approved, a request was submitted, or a GPU appears unused.
4. **Use only the approved scope.** Observe the assigned time, resource limits, and research purpose; ask for extensions in advance.
5. **Protect research priorities.** Security research takes priority. Best-effort computing may be delayed or preempted by administrators.
6. **Avoid disruptions.** Shared configuration, services, security controls, and system software may not be changed without explicit administrator authorization.
7. **Do not share accounts, passwords, private status links, or restricted instructions.** Protect student and research information.
8. **Report problems promptly.** Save checkpoints, release unused reservations, and inform administrators of suspected incidents or failures.
9. **Obtain approval for disruptive experiments.** Exclusive or potentially harmful tests must follow the designated manual review path.
10. **Respect enforcement.** Verified unauthorized resource use may result in immediate job termination and a 24-hour access suspension; repeated violations may lead to longer suspensions and advisor notification.

The reservation service coordinates access. It is not, by itself, a technical isolation system, a job launcher, or proof that an experiment is running. Additional operational rules are provided privately to approved users.
