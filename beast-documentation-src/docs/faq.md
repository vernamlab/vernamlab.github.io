# Frequently asked questions

### Why can't students allocate resources directly in the local Panel?

The Panel is an administrative interface. Uncoordinated changes could conflict with other reservations, disrupt experiments, or damage a shared environment. Students use the request process for a consistent record, fair scheduling, and appropriate accountability.

### Why does my advisor need to approve every booking?

An experiment's duration, resource demand, purpose, or risk may differ from another experiment in the same project. Each ticket needs its own advisor decision.

### Can I reserve several future sessions?

Yes. Submit multiple future requests and ask your advisor to review the tickets together. Each ticket requires its own approval and is still subject to resource availability.

### Does advisor approval mean I can begin working?

No. Wait for confirmed allocation of resources and a specified time window.

### Are all security requests handled manually?

No. A security workload requesting a specific allocation may follow routine scheduling. **Exclusive entire-machine requests require administrator handling.**

### What if my requested resources are unavailable?

You can remain in the queue or request eligible changes through the private monitoring interface. Proposed alternatives are not guaranteed reservations.

### Can I change the requested time without a new advisor approval?

Generally yes, when the same experiment remains within the original resource and duration limits. The scheduler must recheck availability. Expanded or changed work needs further approval.

### Can I cancel a request myself?

If it is in the eligible automatic scheduling queue, use private monitoring. For a request awaiting advisor review, an exclusive-machine request, or a confirmed Panel reservation, contact an administrator.

### Can external collaborators request access?

Approved external collaborators can use the request and notification process. Accounts and operational instructions are provided only through the authorized access process.

### Will I receive an email when resources are reserved?

The monitoring system is designed to email the student when a Panel reservation is created or materially changed, provided the reservation includes a valid student email and synchronization succeeds. Private setup instructions are supplied only after allocation.

### Is a scheduled reservation the same as a running job?

No. A Panel reservation records the allowed resources and time; it does not automatically start a program or guarantee technical isolation.

### Where are the connection and setup instructions?

They are in the restricted operational guide, available after a confirmed reservation. They are intentionally not published on this website.
