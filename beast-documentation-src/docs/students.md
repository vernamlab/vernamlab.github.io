# For students

## Before submitting

Discuss the research task with your advisor and plan the time, computing resources, and any specialized needs. Each reservation covers a defined job or experiment batch. Use the institutionally provided request form, not the administrative GPU Panel or an informal message.

## Submit and track a request

1. Select the workload category and state the purpose, requested time window, and requirements.
2. Provide your own email and your advisor's email, and acknowledge the usage policy.
3. Keep the assigned ticket identifier. The system sends a receipt and asks the advisor to review the request.
4. Wait for a confirmed reservation. Routine eligible requests are considered by the scheduler after advisor approval. Specialized exclusive-use requests require administrator handling.
5. Review the allocation notice for the approved time and resources. The restricted connection guide becomes available only to authorized users with confirmed reservations.

You may submit **several future time slots** rather than waiting until the day of each experiment. Your advisor can review multiple tickets together. Each ticket remains independently approved and subject to availability.

## Monitor progress

Authorized students can request a private status link by email through the monitoring service. It displays their scheduled allocations, queued requests, and available request actions. Do not forward private status links.

## Important boundaries

A reservation is a record of permitted time and resources, **not** an automatic job launch or a guarantee of technical GPU isolation. Follow the execution and safety rules in the restricted guide after confirmation. Contact an administrator before changing a shared environment or attempting disruptive work.
